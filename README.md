# Pure Natural

A modern presentation website developed for a real-world client, showcasing the Pure Natural brand and its natural products.

## About

Pure Natural is a natural products brand founded by Andrei Ardelean. Its product range includes natural perfumes, disinfectants, and insect repellent sprays made with natural ingredients.

The website introduces the brand, showcases its products, and provides essential information for customers.

## Live Site

[Pure Natural](https://pure-natural-online.netlify.app/)

## Features

- Product showcase featuring the Pure Natural range
- Brand overview and essential information
- Introduction to the founder
- Contact information, business address, and social media links
